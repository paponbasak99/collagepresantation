1. HIGH-LEVEL UI PHILOSOPHY & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
- Zero-Framework Architecture: Pure semantic HTML5 and vanilla CSS3 components.
  No React, Vue, or Tailwind. Every style is direct, inspectable, and fast.
- Dual Theme Engine:
  * Light Mode: Warm Stone (#faf8f5) & Pristine Card Surface (#ffffff).
  * Dark Mode: Luxe Obsidian (#070a10) with Sapphire-Slate Card Elevation (#0e1422)
    and luminous cyan-teal accents (#2dd4bf).
- Bilingual Support: Instant live language toggle between English and Bengali (বাংলা)
  powered by `public/js/i18n.js` with `data-i18n` attributes.
- Accessibility System: Built-in A+ font size scaling (Normal, Large, Extra Large)
  and high-contrast mode switcher (`public/js/accessibility.js`).
- Role-Based Portals: Four distinct user journeys:
  * Patient: Discovery, 5-min slot hold, checkout, appointments, slips.
  * Doctor: Live chamber queue, consultation caller, e-prescription writer.
  * Receptionist: Check-in arrivals, cash collection, walk-in token generation.
  * Admin: Clinic KPIs, doctor verification, doctor CRUD, audit trails.

2. GLOBAL LAYOUT & REUSABLE SHELL ELEMENTS
================================================================================
Mount Points:
- `#navbar-mount`: Dynamically rendered by `public/js/navbar.js` on every page.
- `#footer-mount`: Dynamically rendered by `public/js/navbar.js` (renderFooter()).

A. Top Emergency Bar (.emergency-top-bar):
   - Height: Compact 28px, deep midnight obsidian gradient.
   - Live Beacon: Dual-layer pulsating radar sonar dot (.emergency-beacon-dot &
     .emergency-radar-sonar).
   - Live Badge: 24/7 TRIAGE pill tag.
   - Animated Ticker: Vertical sliding track (.emergency-ticker-track) that cycles
     through 3 clinical guarantees every 3.5s (pauses on hover).
   - Quick-Dial Chips: Micro-glass pills with hover lift & icons:
     * Ambulance: +8801711223301 (.emergency-chip-ambulance)
     * Health Call: 16263 (.emergency-chip-health)
     * Emergency: 999 (.emergency-chip-red with pulsing warning icon)

B. Universal Sticky Navbar (.navbar):
   - Brand Logo: Stethoscope icon in teal box + "DocBook" title (.nav-brand).
   - Primary Navigation (.nav-links): Home, Find Doctors, AI Triage modal trigger.
   - Role-Specific Link: Dynamically added when logged in (Appointments, Doctor Panel,
     Reception Desk, or Admin Dashboard).
   - Action Toolbar (.nav-actions):
     * [A+] Font Size Scaler (#font-size-toggle-btn)
     * [বাং / EN] Language Switcher (#lang-toggle-btn)
     * [Moon / Sun] Dark/Light Theme Toggle (#theme-toggle-btn)
     * User Dropdown Pill (#user-menu-btn): Shows avatar, first name, role badge,
       and dropdown menu for profile links and sign out.
   - Mobile Hamburger: 3-line SVG toggle button opening slide-out drawer (#mobile-drawer).

C. Global AI Symptom Checker Modal (#ai-symptom-modal):
   - Available across all pages via the navbar "AI Triage" button or keyboard shortcut.
   - Natural language symptom input with real-time urgency triage detection:
     * Routine / Non-Emergency (Green)
     * High Priority (Amber)
     * Emergency Triage Required (Crimson alert banner)
   - Matches symptoms to clinical departments and recommends verified active doctors.

D. Global Footer (.footer):
   - 4-column responsive grid:
     1. Brand summary & 24/7 Clinical Desk hotline.
     2. Quick Links (Home, Doctors, AI Symptom Checker, Appointments).
     3. Top Specialties (Cardiology, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Dermatology, Orthopedics).
     4. BMDC Accreditation Shield & 100% Verified badge.
   - Sub-footer with copyright and legal links (Terms of Care, Privacy, Refund Policy).

3. COMPLETE PAGE DIRECTORY (FILE BY FILE BREAKDOWN)
================================================================================

1. Homepage (`public/index.html`)
   - Hero Section: Editorial typography heading, live chamber indicator pill,
     4-field unified search bar (Keyword, Specialty dropdown, Hospital dropdown,
     and "Find Doctor" button).
   - Floating Hero Badges: "100% BMDC Verified" and "4.9/5.0 Patient Satisfaction".
   - Trust Metrics Bar: 4 KPI highlight boxes (100% BMDC, 15+ Hospitals, etc.).
   - Specialty Grid: 8 interactive department cards with custom medical SVG icons.
   - Featured Doctors Carousel: Top-rated doctors with fee tags and instant booking.
   - 3-Step "How It Works" Flow: Find Doctor -> 5-Min Hold -> Pay & Get Slip.

2. Doctor Search & Directory (`public/doctors.html`)
   - Left Sidebar: Faceted filters (Search keyword, Specialty checkboxes, Hospital
     dropdown, Gender radio, Consultation fee range slider, Day-of-week chips).
   - Top Filter Bar: Active count indicator, Sort dropdown (Rating, Experience, Fee),
     Grid/List view switch buttons (.view-toggle-btn).
   - Doctor Cards Grid: Doctors rendered with monogram avatar, BMDC shield badge,
     hospital affiliation, fee tags, and direct slot booking button.

3. Doctor Profile & Slot Selection (`public/doctor-profile.html`)
   - Profile Header: Verified avatar, full medical degrees, chamber address, fees.
   - 7-Day Interactive Date Picker: Horizontal sliding calendar pills with day/date.
   - Shift-Based Slot Picker: Grouped by Morning, Afternoon, and Evening shifts.
     Each slot displays start time, status (Available, Held, Booked), and click-to-hold.
   - 5-Minute Hold Notification: Live countdown bar triggered upon slot reservation.
   - Cancellation Waitlist Banner: Displayed when all slots for a date are full.

4. Checkout & Payment (`public/booking.html`)
   - 5-Minute Reservation Hold Bar: Floating top banner with live countdown timer.
     If the timer expires, the slot is automatically released back to the pool.
   - Patient Info Form: Radio for "Myself" vs "Family Member", name, phone, age, gender.
   - Fee Breakdown Summary: Consultation fee, clinic platform charge, total.
   - Payment Selector: Selectable gateway cards (bKash, Nagad, Card, Pay at Clinic).
   - Terms Acceptance Checkbox & Submit Booking Button.

5. Patient Appointments Dashboard (`public/my-appointments.html`)
   - Live Chamber Queue Tracker Widget: Shows current serving token (SL-01), your
     token (SL-04), patients ahead of you, and dynamic estimated wait time.
   - Tab Navigation: Upcoming Appointments, Completed Consultations, Cancelled.
   - Appointment Cards: Doctor monogram, date/time, chamber room, fee status,
     actions (View Slip, Reschedule, Cancel, View Prescription, Write Review).
   - 3-Tier Visual Refund Meter: Shows expected refund % when requesting cancellation.

6. Login & Registration Portal (`public/login.html`)
   - Split Layout: Left editorial brand banner with trust metrics, right interactive form.
   - 1-Click Demo Logins: 4 instant login pills (Admin, Doctor, Receptionist, Patient).
   - Authentication Modes:
     * Password Login: Phone number + password.
     * Passwordless OTP Login: 6-digit auto-advancing segmented OTP input boxes.
     * Patient Registration: Name, phone, password, math CAPTCHA verification.

7. Doctor Chamber Workspace (`public/doctor-panel.html`)
   - Header: Doctor status toggle (Active in Chamber / Away), practicing date filter.
   - Live OPD Queue Manager: Real-time patient list with sequential SL numbers.
   - Consultation Actions: Call Patient -> In Consultation -> Mark Completed.
   - Digital Prescription Writer Modal: Patient diagnosis, symptoms, medication
     dosage table (Drug, Dosage, Duration, Advice), lab test orders, physician seal.
   - Schedule & Leave Manager: Declare day leaves, shift timings, slot blockouts.

8. Reception Front Desk Desk (`public/reception-panel.html`)
   - Practicing Doctors Bar: Live list of doctors currently consulting today.
   - Patient Arrival Check-In: Search booking ID or phone to mark patient as "Arrived".
   - Cash Collection: Record on-spot cash payments for "Pay at Clinic" bookings.
   - Walk-in Token Generator: Instantly generate emergency or walk-in queue tokens.

9. Admin Analytics & Management Dashboard (`public/admin-dashboard.html`)
   - KPI Summary Cards: Total Consultations, Active Doctors, Today Revenue, Holds.
   - Interactive SVG Visualizations: Consultations trend chart, revenue channel pie.
   - Doctor Verification Table: Inspect BMDC registration credentials, approve or
     suspend practicing licenses.
   - System Audit Trail: Live log viewer showing medical record access, logins, holds.
   - CSV Export: 1-click download of appointments, financial ledgers, and doctors.

10. Printable Slips & Verification Pages:
    - `public/appointment-slip.html`: Print-optimized medical token with sequential SL
      badge, QR code verification graphic, chamber directions, and clinic rules.
    - `public/prescription-slip.html`: Printable e-prescription with doctor seal,
      BMDC stamp, medication tables, diagnostic orders, and verification QR code.
    - `public/verify-slip.html`: Public verification landing page displayed when scanning
      any QR code with a smartphone camera.

4. DESIGN SYSTEM TOKENS (COLORS, TYPOGRAPHY, ELEVATION)

   --color-muted               #ede8df (Divider)        #1a2338 (Muted Slate)

   --color-teal-700 (Brand)    #0d7a71 (Clinical Teal)  #0d9488 (Emerald Teal)
   --color-teal-500 (Glow)     #14b8a6                  #2dd4bf (Luminous Cyan)
   --color-ink-950 (Headings)  #0a0e14                  #ffffff (Pure Crisp White)
   --color-ink-900 (Titles)    #111827                  #f1f5f9
   --color-ink-700 (Body)      #374151                  #cbd5e1 (Zero-Fatigue Body)
   --color-ink-500 (Meta)      #6b7280                  #64748b
   --color-border              #e7e4dc                  rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)
   --color-border-strong       #b8b1a3                  rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)
   --color-danger              #b91c1c                  #f87171 (Soft Crimson)
   --color-warning             #b45309                  #fbbf24 (Soft Amber Gold)
   --color-success             #15803d                  #34d399 (Clinical Emerald)
   --color-info                #0369a1                  #38bdf8 (Sky Azure)

B. Typography:
   - Display Font: 'Fraunces', serif (Editorial, warm, clinical authority).
   - UI Body Font: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif (Clean geometric modern UI).
   - Bengali Font: 'Hind Siliguri', sans-serif (Pixel-perfect Bangla script).
   - Numbers: Tabular lining figures (font-feature-settings: 'tnum') on prices,
     slots, dates, queue numbers, and timers.

C. Shadows & Elevation:
   - --shadow-xs:       Subtle component boundary.
   - --shadow-sm:       Small elevation for buttons and chips.
   - --shadow-md:       Default card elevation with top-edge hairline highlight.
   - --shadow-lg:       Elevated dropdowns, hero search box, and feature cards.
   - --shadow-floating: Heavy modal and slide-out drawer diffused shadow.

5. INTERACTIVE WIDGETS & MEDICAL SPECIALTY COMPONENTS
================================================================================

1. 5-Minute Atomic Slot Hold Bar (.countdown-bar):
   - Live visual countdown bar indicating temporary slot reservation.
   - Background sweeper releases abandoned holds every 30 seconds.
   - Concurrency guarantee: Zero double-booking via SQLite BEGIN IMMEDIATE.

2. Live OPD Chamber Queue Tracker (.queue-tracker):
   - Shows sequential patient position: "Now Serving SL-02 | Your SL-05".
   - Shows dynamic remaining patients: "3 patients ahead of you (~24 min wait)".
   - Pulsing emerald live chamber dot.

3. Doctor Monogram Avatar (.doctor-monogram-avatar):
   - Algorithmic 2-letter physician initials with verified check shield badge.
   - Harmonized translucent glass palette in dark mode.

4. 3-Stage Cancellation Refund Tier Bar:
   - Visual tier meter indicating refund policy:
     * > 24 hours: 90% refund
     * 6 to 24 hours: 50% refund
     * < 6 hours: 0% non-refundable
   - Automatically highlights the current active tier based on appointment time.

5. Segmented OTP Verification Boxes:
   - 6 standalone single-digit input fields with auto-advance, backspace retreat,
     and copy-paste distribution.
